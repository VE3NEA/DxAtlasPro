DX Atlas Pro

Refund Policy

Note: DX Atlas Pro is in beta testing, and each beta is free until its expiry date. Registration keys go on sale with the first public release, v.1.0; this policy applies from then on.

Try before you buy

DX Atlas Pro can be used free of charge for 30 days, with no function disabled or limited. Please use this trial to make sure the program meets your needs and works on your computer before you buy a registration key.

When a refund is given

Within 30 days of purchase, an order is refunded:

  • when it was a duplicate purchase, for example the same key bought twice by mistake;
  • when the program has a technical problem on your computer that cannot be resolved.

How to ask for a refund

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from, which is the merchant of record for your order:

  • PayPro Global: through its customer support, or the customer control panel whose link is in your order email;
  • BMT Micro: through its customer support.

Give the store your order id, which is in its order email, and the reason for the refund. The refund is paid to your original payment method; the time it takes to reach your account depends on the store and your bank.

For a technical problem, please write to support@dxatlas.com first, so that we can try to resolve it.

After a refund

The registration key of a refunded order is revoked, and later versions of DX Atlas Pro do not accept it.
