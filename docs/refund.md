# Refund Policy

## Try before you buy

DX Atlas Pro can be used free of charge for 30 days, with no function disabled or limited. Please
use this trial to make sure the program meets your needs and works on your computer before you
buy a registration key.

## When a refund is given

Within 30 days of purchase, an order is refunded:

- when it was a **duplicate purchase**, for example the same key bought twice by mistake;
- when the program has a **technical problem** on your computer that cannot be resolved.

## How to ask for a refund

Refunds are handled by the store, [BMT Micro](https://www.bmtmicro.com/), which is the merchant of
record for your order: ask through its customer support.

Give the store your order id, which is in its order email, and the reason for the refund. The
refund is paid to your original payment method; the time it takes to reach your account depends
on the store and your bank.

For a technical problem, please write to [support@dxatlas.com](mailto:support@dxatlas.com) first,
so that we can try to resolve it.

## After a refund

The registration key of a refunded order is revoked, and later versions of DX Atlas Pro do not
accept it.
