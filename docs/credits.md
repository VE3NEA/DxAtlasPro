## Ionospheric Model and Indices

- [IRI-2026](https://irimodel.org/), the International Reference Ionosphere, © 2016
  International Reference Ionosphere — IRI license. The model is the work of the IRI Working
  Group of COSPAR and URSI; DX Atlas Pro uses a Pascal port of its routines and its coefficient
  files.
- [IGRF-14](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/international-geomagnetic-reference-field), the
  International Geomagnetic Reference Field, by IAGA Division V, Working Group V-MOD
- Geomagnetic indices Kp, ap and Ap from [GFZ Helmholtz Centre for Geosciences](https://kp.gfz.de/),
  CC BY 4.0, converted to IRI's `apf107.dat` format with the sunspot number column removed:
  Matzka, J., Stolle, C., Yamazaki, Y., Bronkalla, O. and Morschhauser, A. (2021), The
  geomagnetic Kp index and derived indices of geomagnetic activity, *Space Weather*,
  [doi:10.1029/2020SW002641](https://doi.org/10.1029/2020SW002641); Matzka, J., Bronkalla, O.,
  Tornow, K., Elger, K. and Stolle, C. (2021), Geomagnetic Kp index, V. 1.0, GFZ Data Services,
  [doi:10.5880/Kp.0001](https://doi.org/10.5880/Kp.0001)
- F10.7 solar flux from the Dominion Radio Astrophysical Observatory and Natural Resources
  Canada, as provided in GFZ's file
- CCIR/ITU-R numerical ionospheric maps, as distributed with
  [VOACAP](https://www.voacap.com/)
