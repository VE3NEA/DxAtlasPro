# Privacy Policy

This policy describes the personal data that Afreet Software Inc ("we", "us"), the publisher of
DX Atlas Pro, receives and how it is used.

## The program

DX Atlas Pro sends no personal data. It has no telemetry, crash reporting, update check or online
licence check: the registration key is checked on your computer, offline.

The program's only internet traffic is anonymous downloads of public space-weather data:

- the aurora forecast from NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center, while the aurora overlay is
  shown;
- geomagnetic and solar indices from GFZ Helmholtz Centre for Geosciences and from irimodel.org.

Like any download, these requests reveal your computer's IP address to those servers. They carry
nothing else about you.

The program's API for other software listens only on your own computer (127.0.0.1) and is not
reachable from the network. The program's Buy and Download commands open this website in your
browser.

## Purchases

When you buy a registration key, you enter your name, email address and billing details at the
checkout of the store, [PayPro Global](https://payproglobal.com/) or
[BMT Micro](https://www.bmtmicro.com/). The store processes your payment under its own privacy
policy; we never see your card or payment details.

The store passes the order to us. We use it as follows:

- **the licensee name and the order id** are recorded by our key server with the key issued, to
  find the key of an order when it is refunded, and revoke it;
- **your name, email address and country**, in the store's order notification, are kept with our
  sales records, to answer your support requests and as required by tax and accounting law.

We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone, and do not use it for marketing.

## This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses Google Analytics to count visits and the pages
read. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your IP address and browser details under
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); you can block it with your
browser's privacy settings or an ad blocker. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses under the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Email

If you write to us, we keep your message and email address to answer it.

## Your rights

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by
writing to [support@dxatlas.com](mailto:support@dxatlas.com). Records we must keep by law are
kept for the period the law requires. For data held by a store, contact that store.
