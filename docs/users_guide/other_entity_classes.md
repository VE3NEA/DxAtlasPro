# Other Entity Classes

## FFMA Grid Squares

**FFMA Grid Squares** in the **Amateur Radio** menu draws the 488 grid squares of the ARRL's Fred
Fish Memorial Award, the squares of the 48 contiguous United States, each labelled with its
locator, such as **CM79**. The layer is off by default.

The **FFMA grid squares** layer of the [Lists Panel](lists_panel.md) lists the squares, and allows
you to keep lists of the squares worked and confirmed on 6 metres; see [Award Tracking](award_tracking.md).
