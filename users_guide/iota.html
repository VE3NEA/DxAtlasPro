IOTA

IOTA Groups in the Amateur Radio menu draws the 1,178 island groups of the Islands on the Air programme, each as the rectangle that bounds its islands, labelled with its reference, such as AF-001. The layer is off by default.

Hover the mouse over a reference to see the group's name. The rectangles of neighbouring groups overlap, so the Item Details of a point list every IOTA group whose rectangle holds it.

The IOTA groups layer of the Lists Panel lists the groups by reference and name, and keeps lists of the groups worked or confirmed; see Award Tracking.

The IOTA groups and their rectangles are used with the permission of the IOTA Committee. For the programme and its rules, see www.rsgbiota.org.
