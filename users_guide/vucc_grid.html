VUCC Grid Squares

VUCC Grid Squares in the Amateur Radio menu draws the Maidenhead locator grid, used by the ARRL's VHF/UHF Century Club award and by VHF contesters:

  • at a world view, the 324 fields, 20 by 10 degrees, labelled AA to RR;
  • zoomed in, the 32,400 squares, 2 by 1 degrees, labelled with their four characters, such as FN03.

The grid is drawn by the program as you zoom, so it is sharp at every scale.

The status bar always shows the 6-character locator of the point under the mouse; see Status Bar.
