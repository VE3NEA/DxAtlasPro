The Home Location

The home location is your own location. It is used in several places:

  • both maps mark it with a house. The mark is switched on and off by Home in the Layers menu;
  • the status bar shows the bearing and the distance from it to the point under the mouse;
  • the azimuth and distance lines radiate from it;
  • the azimuthal map is centred on it when you switch to that projection, and again whenever the home location changes;
  • the Clock panel shows its sunrise and sunset, and can lock the time to them.

There are three ways to set it:

  • right-click the map at your location and pick Set Home Location Here. A message box shows the latitude, longitude and grid square of the point; click Yes to make it home;
  • enter the latitude and longitude in the Home Location section of the Settings Dialog;
  • enter your 4- or 6-character Maidenhead grid square in the same section; the latitude and longitude move to the centre of that square.

A click with keys of your choice can also set the home location: assign them to Set as Home in the Mouse Clicks section of the Settings dialog.

The Home Button

The Home button on the map toolbar zooms the map out to the whole world:

  • the rectangular map is centred on latitude and longitude 0;
  • the azimuthal map and the globe are centred on the home location.
