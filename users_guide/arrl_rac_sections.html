ARRL/RAC Sections

ARRL Sections in the Amateur Radio menu draws the 85 sections of the ARRL and of Radio Amateurs of Canada, used in the ARRL Sweepstakes, Field Day and other contests, each labelled with its code, such as EMA or ONS. The layer is off by default.

Hover the mouse over a code to see the section's name. The Item Details of a section list its call area.

The ARRL/RAC sections layer of the Lists Panel lists the sections by code and name, and keeps lists of the sections worked; see Award Tracking.
