  • Geomagnetic indices Kp, ap and Ap from GFZ Helmholtz Centre for Geosciences, CC BY 4.0, converted to IRI's apf107.dat format with the sunspot number column removed: Matzka, J., Stolle, C., Yamazaki, Y., Bronkalla, O. and Morschhauser, A. (2021), The geomagnetic Kp index and derived indices of geomagnetic activity, Space Weather, doi:10.1029/2020SW002641; Matzka, J., Bronkalla, O., Tornow, K., Elger, K. and Stolle, C. (2021), Geomagnetic Kp index, V. 1.0, GFZ Data Services, doi:10.5880/Kp.0001
